About me

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the University of Southern Denmark. My research combines wearable and affective computing, physiological signal processing, and machine learning.

I focus especially on recognizing stress and emotion from unobtrusive wearable sensors across laboratory and everyday settings. My work also considers multimodal learning, privacy-preserving methods, and explainable health AI.

Research interests

  • Wearable computing
  • Stress recognition
  • Affective computing
  • Physiological signals
  • Self-supervised learning
  • Explainable health AI
